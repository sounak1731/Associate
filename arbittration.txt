# Associate Demo Matter
## Mehra Exports Pvt. Ltd. v. CloudServ Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**Why this matter:** B2B payment default under an MSA. Every lawyer in India has seen one. It has a contract, unpaid invoices, emails, a legal notice, and a live limitation question — which means it triggers every single feature of Associate in a natural, believable sequence. It's not exotic. It's Tuesday.

**One-line brief:** Mehra Exports engaged CloudServ for a ₹48L cloud migration. CloudServ delivered partial work, raised full invoices, went silent after a formal demand email. Mehra wants their ₹28.32L back. The MSA has an arbitration clause. Limitation is 3 years from breach — 21 months have elapsed.

```
This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of

Mehra Exports Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of
India, having its registered office at 14-B, Nehru Place,

CloudServ Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the
laws of India, having its registered office at 402, Prestige Tower,
MG Road, Bengaluru – 560001 ("Service Provider")

SCOPE OF SERVICES
CloudServ shall provide cloud migration, infrastructure setup, and
managed services as detailed in Schedule A. Total project value:
₹48,00,000 (Rupees Forty-Eight Lakhs), payable in four instalments
upon milestone completion.

PAYMENT TERMS
Client shall pay each instalment within 30 days of receipt of
invoice, subject to milestone sign-off by Client's designated
project manager.

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
CloudServ's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not
exceed the amounts paid by Client in the preceding three months.

DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute shall first be referred to senior management of both
parties for 30 days. Failing resolution, disputes shall be referred
to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996,
before a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual consent, seated at
New Delhi.

GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

Signed:
Rahul Mehra, Director — Mehra Exports Pvt. Ltd.
Vikram Sinha, CEO — CloudServ Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Date: 12 January 2024
```
