Master Services Agreement

between Pranos Technologies Inc. & Apex India Private Limited · dated 10 April 2026

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date set forth above, by and between Pranos Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 500 Howard Street, San Francisco, California ("Vendor"), and Apex India Private Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of India with its registered office at Prestige Atrium, Bengaluru 560001 ("Client").

Recitals

Vendor is engaged in the business of providing cloud-based legal workflow software and related services.1

Client wishes to procure, and Vendor wishes to provide, such services on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

The parties agree that this Agreement shall govern all future Statements of Work executed between them.

1. Definitions

1.1 "Services" means the cloud-based software services described in Schedule A, together with any ancillary services Vendor agrees to provide from time to time.

1.2 "Client Data" means any data, including personal data as defined under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, uploaded to or processed through the Services by Client or its authorised users.2

1.3 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that would reasonably be understood as confidential given the nature of the information.

6. Fees and Payment

6.1 Client shall pay all invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month from the due date until paid in full.3

8. Indemnification

8.2 Each party shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other party from any and all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses of any nature, in an amount not to exceed one and one-half times (1.5×) the annual fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months, arising from breach of this Agreement.4

11. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales, without regard to its conflict-of-law provisions.5 Disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally resolved by arbitration under the Rules of the International Chamber of Commerce, seated in Singapore, before a panel of three arbitrators.

14. Data Processing

14.3 Vendor may process, store and transfer Client Data to any jurisdiction deemed necessary for service delivery, without prior notice to Client. Client hereby consents to such transfers in advance.6

16. General

16.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements, whether written or oral, relating to the subject matter hereof.

16.2 No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.
