                           LEGAL NOTICE

Dear Sir/Madam,

We are the Advocates for and on behalf of our client,
M/s Mehra Exports Pvt. Ltd., having its registered office
at 14-B, Nehru Place, New Delhi – 110019 (hereinafter
referred to as "our Client"), and we address you at

1.  BACKGROUND

1.1 Our Client is a company duly incorporated under the
    Companies Act, 2013, engaged in the business of exports.

1.2 On 12th January 2024, our Client entered into a Master
    Services Agreement ("MSA") with your company
    (CloudServ Technologies Pvt. Ltd., hereinafter "you"
    or "your company") for the provision of cloud migration,
    infrastructure setup, and managed services, at a total
    contract value of ₹48,00,000 (Rupees Forty-Eight Lakhs),
    payable in four equal milestone-linked instalments.

1.3 In accordance with the MSA, our Client duly paid the
    first instalment of ₹14,16,000 (including GST) upon
    satisfactory completion of Milestone 1 (Infrastructure
    Assessment & Architecture Design), vide payment dated
    8th March 2024.


2.  BREACH AND FAILURE TO PERFORM

2.1 You raised Invoice No. CSTPL/2024/002 dated 3rd April
    2024 for ₹14,16,000 towards Milestone 2 (Cloud Migration).
    However, at the time of raising the said invoice, Milestone
    2 deliverables were incomplete — specifically, the migration
    of the ERP module, which constitutes the core deliverable
    under Milestone 2, was only approximately 40% complete.

2.2 In accordance with Clause 3.1 of the MSA, payment of any
    milestone instalment is subject to sign-off by our Client's
    designated Project Manager confirming satisfactory completion.
    Our Client's Project Manager withheld sign-off and
    communicated the reasons in writing vide email dated
    22nd March 2024, a copy of which is enclosed herewith
    as Annexure A.

2.3 Despite the above, you neither completed the outstanding
    Milestone 2 deliverables nor provided a revised timeline
    as requested.

2.4 You further raised Invoice No. CSTPL/2024/003 dated 1st July
    2024 for ₹14,16,000 towards Milestone 3 (Data Sync &
    Handover). As of the date of this notice, no work has
    been commenced on Milestone 3, as evidenced by our Client's
    project tracker. The said invoice was raised unilaterally
    and in violation of Clause 3.2 of the MSA, which expressly
    prohibits the raising of invoices prior to commencement
    and submission of Deliverables.

2.5 Our Client addressed a formal written demand to your company
    vide email dated 15th August 2024, calling upon you to
    complete the outstanding deliverables or respond within
    14 days. Your company's sole response, vide email dated
    16th August 2024, was that your team had been "occupied
    with other projects" and would "revert shortly." No
    substantive response has been received since.

2.6 Your conduct constitutes a clear and material breach of
    the MSA, including but not limited to:

    (a) Failure to complete Milestone 2 deliverables despite
        receipt of prior payment for Milestone 1;

    (b) Raising Invoice 002 without milestone sign-off, in
        violation of Clause 3.2 of the MSA;

    (c) Raising Invoice 003 for work not commenced, in
        violation of Clause 3.2 of the MSA;

    (d) Failure to respond substantively to our Client's
        formal demand of 15th August 2024.


3.  AMOUNT DUE AND PAYABLE

3.1 In light of the above, our Client is entitled to the
    following:

    (a) A declaration that neither Invoice 002 nor Invoice 003
        is payable in the absence of corresponding milestone
        completion and sign-off;

    AND / OR IN THE ALTERNATIVE

    (b) Damages for partial failure of consideration in respect
        of the contract value paid (₹14,16,000 for Milestone 1)
        for which full contracted deliverables have not been
        received;

    (c) Without prejudice to the above, in the event this
        dispute proceeds to arbitration, our Client will claim
        the full sum of ₹28,32,000 (Rupees Twenty-Eight Lakhs
        Thirty-Two Thousand) representing the aggregate of
        Invoice 002 (₹14,16,000) and Invoice 003 (₹14,16,000)
        which have been wrongfully raised and remain unpaid,
        together with interest at 18% per annum from the
        respective invoice due dates, and costs.


4.  NOTICE AND DEMAND

4.1 In light of the foregoing, we hereby call upon you to,
    within FIFTEEN (15) DAYS of receipt of this notice:

    (a) Complete all outstanding Milestone 2 deliverables
        to the satisfaction of our Client's Project Manager
        and resubmit for sign-off; OR

    (b) Pay to our Client the sum of ₹28,32,000 (Rupees
        Twenty-Eight Lakhs Thirty-Two Thousand) in full and
        final settlement of all claims arising under the MSA;

    failing which, our Client shall, without further notice
    or intimation to you, initiate arbitration proceedings
    under Clause 9 of the MSA read with Section 8 of the
    Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and shall
    seek recovery of the aforesaid amount together with
    interest at 18% per annum and full costs of the
    arbitration proceedings.

4.2 Please note that this notice shall constitute the
    written notice of dispute required under Clause 9.1
    of the MSA, and the 30-day good faith resolution
    period thereunder is hereby invoked. The 15-day
    period specified above runs concurrently therewith.


5.  RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

Our Client expressly reserves all rights and remedies
available to it under the MSA, in equity, and under
applicable law, including the right to seek additional
damages, interest, and costs as may be determined by
